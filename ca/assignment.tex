\section*{Objective}
You are required to create a \textbf{presentation of approximately 10 frames} on an advanced topic in computer graphics that has not been covered in class. This assignment is designed to broaden your understanding of real-world techniques, cutting-edge research, and industry practices in modern computer graphics.

\section*{Instructions and Guidelines}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=0.6cm]
  \item \textbf{There are 50 topics} to choose from. The full list is available on the \href{https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1u53Q3IpKolDNdgppDxxXberWJHSxwfAub1AxBailzJA/edit?usp=sharing}{topic selection sheet}.
  \item \textbf{Each topic may be assigned to at most 3 students.} This can be:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Three individuals working independently on the same topic
      \item A group of two and one individual
      \item A group of three
    \end{itemize}
  \item If working in a group, all members must contribute equally. \textbf{Group size cannot exceed 3}.

  \item \textbf{Your presentation must include:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Clear and concise explanation of the topic
      \item Diagrams, examples, and visuals wherever appropriate
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Recommended Tools:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Use \textbf{Beamer} with the LaTeX \href{https://github.com/risenfromashes/cse409-resources}{preamble} provided for course slides
      \item You may use this \href{https://www.overleaf.com/read/sfqpwxcdrpny#497ff0}{Overleaf project} to get started
      \item Use \textbf{TikZ} for diagrams when applicable
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{You must record a video presentation (max duration: 10 minutes)} where all members present equally.

  \item Submit the following files:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Beamer \texttt{.tex} (or other) source files and images
      \item Exported PDF or PPTX of your slides
      \item A video file of the presentation (MP4, MKV, etc.)
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{All materials must be uploaded to this \href{https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ML6WZruHu89Cj47HePLaejBWfE4kCOdx?usp=sharing}{Google Drive folder}:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Create a subfolder titled with your \textbf{group name} (or your student ID if individual) and place all files there
      \item Submit a link to your folder in a text file in moodle
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Topic selection:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Visit the \href{https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1u53Q3IpKolDNdgppDxxXberWJHSxwfAub1AxBailzJA/edit?usp=sharing}{Google Sheet}
      \item Write your group name or student ID beside the topic you are choosing in the “Topic Sheet"
      \item If you are in a group, also list group members and their student IDs in the “Groups" sheet
      \item The sheet is publicly editable. Manage it among yourselves. The final sheet will be treated as the authoritative record. No further mediation will be done.
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Bonus Opportunity:} Submit reviews of \textbf{three presentations on distinct topics} (excluding your own) for up to \textbf{+5 bonus marks}.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Reviews must be short (\textless{} 500 words), honest, and constructive.
      \item Reviews must be written in your own words and submitted in the “Review” sheet in the spreadsheet.
      \item Flattering, superficial, or generative AI-based reviews will receive a penalty of \textbf{-10 marks}.
      \item \textbf{You must submit your own presentation} to be eligible to submit reviews.
    \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Failure to follow instructions will result in 0 marks.}

  \item \textbf{Deadline: \textbf{31 July 2025}}.
\end{enumerate}

  \item Bonus for reviews (see above) \hfill (5)
\end{itemize}
